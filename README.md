# 12-Week Athletic Training & Nutrition Plan

A professional, athlete-grade 12-week training and nutrition management application designed to help you break through plateaus, improve athletic performance, and optimize body composition.

## 🚀 Overview
This application is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that serves as your personal fitness coach. It tracks your 12-week training progress, monitors nutritional intake, and helps you manage your body composition goals with high-intensity interval training (HIIT) and sport-specific movements.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Structured 12-Week Roadmap**: Divided into three phases (Foundation, Strength & Explosion, and Definition).
- **Interactive Progress Tracking**: Log daily workouts, including running, strength, and core exercises.
- **Nutritional Management**: Monitor your daily calorie, protein, carbohydrate, and fat intake to ensure optimal recovery.
- **Offline Capability**: Built as a PWA, allowing you to access your plans and logs anytime, even without an internet connection.
- **Joint Health Support**: Includes dedicated recovery plans to protect your knees and ankles during high-intensity training.

## 📱 Installation (Android)
1. Open the website link in your **Chrome** browser on your Android device.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu** in the top-right corner.
3. Select **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install App"**.
4. The 💪 icon will appear on your home screen for easy access.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Web**: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Vanilla).
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Implemented with `sw.js` for offline caching and `manifest.json` for native-like installation.

## 📋 Training Philosophy
The plan focuses on:
- **Quality over Quantity**: High-intensity training with conscious eccentric control.
- **Recovery**: Prioritizing joint health and nutrition to support long-term performance.
- **Consistency**: Tracking metrics like body fat, waist circumference, and exercise performance to drive results.
